## 1. Physical Verification Standard

Per Section 29, a production QR code cannot be claimed as "VERIFIED" until actual physical prints are tested in field conditions.

### Test Matrix Parameters:
1. **Scanning Distances**:
   - 15 cm (Close range)
   - 30 cm (Normal interaction range)
   - 50 cm (Arm length / garden plot railing range)
2. **Angles**:
   - 0° (Perpendicular / direct)
   - 15° (Slight tilt)
   - 30° (Moderate perspective)
   - 45° (Extreme perspective)
3. **Lighting Conditions**:
   - Indoor ambient light
   - Outdoor daylight (bright sun)
   - Lower light / shaded canopy
4. **Physical Media**:
   - Laminated weather-resistant garden sign
   - Matte acrylic garden marker

* **Status**: **`NOT TESTED`**.
